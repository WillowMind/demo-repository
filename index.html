Why Willow

Some things are hard to say out loud. Willow gives you somewhere to start before you're ready to.

Many people wait a long time before reaching out. Often it isn't because they don't need support, but because asking feels like being seen before they're ready.

Willow is built for that in-between time. You stay in control of how much of yourself you share, from the first day to the day you decide to see a licensed therapist, if that day comes.

How it works

Four steps, at whatever pace suits you.

  1. 1

    Join under an alias

    All you need is an alias and an email address. No real name, phone number or photo.

  2. 2

    Get something for today

    Tell Willow how you feel and get a song, a short story and a coping tip chosen for it.

  3. 3

    Talk to a counsellor

    Book a trained counsellor and talk under your alias or your name. You can switch before any session.

  4. 4

    Move to therapy when ready

    If you want more support, move to a licensed therapist and choose exactly what comes with you.

What we believe

Six ideas Willow is built on, each with a short story.

    Questions

    Questions people ask before they join.

    Do I need to use my real name?

    No. You only need an alias and an email address. Your email keeps your account secure and is never shown to counsellors.

    Is Willow therapy?

    Willow is a first step: comfort content and conversations with trained counsellors. When you want clinical support, Willow helps you move to a licensed therapist, only if and when you choose to.

    Who can see what I share?

    Only the people you choose. Counsellors see your alias unless you share more, and nothing goes to a therapist without your approval.

    What if I need help right now?

    Willow is not an emergency or crisis service. If you're in immediate danger or thinking about ending your life, contact your local emergency number or a crisis helpline straight away, or go to your nearest hospital.

    Can I delete my account?

    Yes, at any time from your settings. Your data is deleted with it.

    How much does it cost?

    Pricing is being finalised during early access. People on the waitlist hear about it first.
